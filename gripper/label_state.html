Rule: open = fingers apart / opening and not holding anything; closed = fingers together or clamped on an object; unknown = fingers hidden/blurred. closed→open: put the change point on the FIRST frame the fingers START to separate (the release moment of the spec, not when the gap is already wide - step back frame by frame). open→closed: the frame the fingers touch each other or the object. Striped band = model guess (accept it by leaving it, edits turn into yours; Enter makes everything yours).
